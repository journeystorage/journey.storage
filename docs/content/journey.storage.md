## SECTION 3: BRAND POSITIONING

- **Elements:** Section headline (H2), body copy (2–3 short paragraphs)
- **Image needed:** Yes — one supporting visual. Subject defined in Page Design Spec.
- **Intent:** The brand's soul on display. Adapts the manifesto's emotional core for public use — names the universal truth (there's a moment nobody talks about) and positions Journey as the answer without being heavy-handed. The visitor should feel understood, not sold to.

**Copy:**

> **H2:** Not a warehouse. A partner. 
>
> There's a moment in every transition that nobody talks about. It's not the new apartment, the grand opening, or the first day of something new. It's the moment right before — when you've decided where you're going, but you're not there yet. 
>
> That's where Journey lives. 
>
> We're not here to rent you a unit. We're here so you can focus on what actually needs your attention — the move, the business, the family, the fresh start. 

**Note:** This copy adapts the brand manifesto's energy but does NOT reproduce it literally (per brand manual: manifesto is internal-only).

## SECTION 4: HOW IT WORKS

- **Elements:** Section headline (H2), 5 numbered steps (each: bold title + one-line description)
- **Icons needed:** Yes — one per step. Style defined in Design System.
- **Intent:** Shift from emotional to functional — but still through Journey's lens. Each step is written from the customer's perspective, not Journey's. Every step tells the customer what THEY do and what THEY don't have to deal with.

**Copy:**

> **H2:** Storage without the friction. 
>
> **Step 1: Find your space**
> Browse available units and reserve online. No phone calls, no office visits.
>
> **Step 2: Verify in seconds**
> Quick digital identity verification. No paperwork, no waiting.
>
> **Step 3: Sign and get your key**
> Digital lease. Instant access code delivered to your phone.
>
> **Step 4: Arrive and move in**
> Gates open automatically. Your unit is ready. Any hour, any day. 
>
> **Step 5: You're in control**
> Manage everything from your phone. Pay, upgrade, or move out — zero friction.

## SECTION 5: DIFFERENTIATORS

- **Elements:** Section headline (H2), 4 content blocks (each: bold statement as hook + 1–2 sentence expansion)
- **Image needed:** Yes — at least one. Subject defined in Page Design Spec.
- **Intent:** Bold promises. The soul differentiators come first (empathy, human service), then experience (transparency), then product (security/access). This hierarchy is what separates Journey from every competitor. The copy is direct and specific — real statements about real industry practices.

**Copy:**

> **H2:** A different kind of storage company. 
>
> **Block 1 (Soul):**
> **We don't auction your life when times get hard.** 
> Missed a payment? We'll work with you. Empathy isn't an exception to our policy — it is the policy.
>
> **Block 2 (Experience):**
> **No hidden fees. No traps. No surprises.**
> Clear pricing. Month-to-month. Cancel when you want. We earn your stay, not your lock-in. 
>
> **Block 3 (Soul):**
> **Talk to a real person.**
> Not a chatbot. Not a form. Someone who understands you're in the middle of one of the most stressful moments of your life — and knows how to help. 
>
> **Block 4 (Product):**
> **Smart, safe, and always open.**
> 24/7 access. Smart locks. Climate control. Live monitoring. Security isn't a feature — it's trust made physical. `⚑ REVIEW — "trust made physical" from brand manual`

## SECTION 6: LOCATIONS

- **Elements:** Section headline (H2), subline (paragraph), U.S. map with scattered pins (decorative/aspirational), ZIP code capture form (input + button), helper text
- **Image needed:** No — the map IS the visual element. Map is a custom-built component (SVG or similar), not a photograph.
- **Intent:** Visual showstopper. Communicates national ambition without naming specific cities. Pins are aspirational — they say "Journey is going everywhere." The ZIP capture turns excitement into geographic demand data. The visitor should feel like they're watching something big take shape.

**Copy:**

> **H2:** Our locations are finding home. 
>
> **Subline:** We're building something new across America. Our first facilities open soon — and this is just the beginning. 
>
> **Form prompt:** Want Journey in your city? 
>
> **Form fields:** [ZIP Code input] [Notify Me — button]
>
> **Helper text:** We'll let you know the moment Journey arrives near you.

**Map spec:**
- Stylized U.S. map (custom, not Google Maps)
- 15–25 scattered pins across various states — not specific cities, not labeled
- Purely aspirational — communicates national scale
- Visual style defined in Design System / Page Design Spec

## SECTION 7: FOUNDED BY

- **Elements:** Section headline (H2), body copy (2–3 sentences), portrait photo, two text links to subdomains
- **Image needed:** Yes — actual photo of Jonah M. Hall (from existing materials)
- **Intent:** Trust signal for consumers. NOT a full bio — that lives on the subdomains. The headline establishes credibility ("built this industry") and then reframes it as a values decision ("walked away to build it right"). Most consumers will scroll past reassured; investors and operators will click through.

**Copy:**

> **H2:** Founded by someone who built this industry — and walked away to build it right. 
>
> **Body:** Jonah M. Hall has spent nearly a decade in self-storage — acquiring, building, and operating over $200M in assets across 17 facilities. He left it all behind to start Journey: a company built on the belief that storage should serve people, not the other way around. 
>
> **Link 1:** Learn more about our investment platform → `links to direct.journey.storage`
> **Link 2:** Explore our consulting division → `links to advisory.journey.storage`

## SECTION 8: WAITLIST

- **Elements:** Section headline (H2), subline (paragraph), form (4 fields + submit button), social media links, post-submission success message
- **Image needed:** No
- **Intent:** The close. After the emotional journey through the page, the visitor arrives here ready to act. The copy doesn't push — it invites. "Be the first" creates exclusivity. The form is minimal. ZIP code is required (most valuable data point for expansion planning). Phone is optional and clearly labeled to reduce friction.

**Copy:**

> **H2:** Be the first to experience Journey. 
>
> **Subline:** Sign up and we'll let you know when Journey is ready in your area — plus early access, updates, and a few things we're only sharing with our first community. 
>
> **Form fields:**
> - Name (required)
> - Email (required)
> - ZIP Code (required)
> - Phone (optional — labeled "Optional — for SMS updates")
>
> **CTA button:** Join the Waitlist
>
> **Social links label:** Follow the journey.
> **Platforms:** Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook
>
> **Success message (after form submit):**
> You're on the list. We'll be in touch soon. Welcome to the journey.
