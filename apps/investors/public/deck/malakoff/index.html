The market

Demand is arriving. Supply is not.

The last three years absorbed a record wave of new storage. That wave is over. Deliveries are projected to fall roughly 60% from the 2023 peak, leaving an operator who already owns a new building with very few new neighbors.

Annual new self-storage supply · nationwide · facilities New buildExpansion
−62%Deliveries, 2023 to 2029

Absorption now outpaces new deliveries in heavily built markets. In markets like Malakoff, an operator can push rate without new competition diluting demand.

20%+Portfolio value premium

Regional portfolios trade to institutional buyers at compressed cap rates: a 5% cap versus 6% is roughly 20% more value. Malakoff is underwritten to a 6.00% exit on its own; a portfolio exit is upside, not the plan.

Demand drivers · trade area

Lake-home and second-home ownership around Cedar Creek Lake drives seasonal and recreational storage, with the area’s estimated median home value nearly quadrupling over the last 20 years.

Years one and two run below debt service. That is the plan, and it is funded.

A building bought empty does not cover its interest in year one. The $850,000 raised at close covers the gap while the loan is interest-only for 36 months. By Year 3 the property covers its debt 1.65 times over; Year 4 is stabilized. Read the negative Year 1 as a funded lease-up, not a surprise.
