The deal in one minute

Five reasons this works.

Most storage offerings ask you to underwrite a story. This one asks you to underwrite a building that already exists, a price that is already set, and an operator with a repeatable playbook for filling it.

01

Basis below cost

Purchased from the developer for less than was spent to build it, despite it being built without a market third-party general contractor margin.

02

Familiar lease-up

With experience leasing up from zero twenty times over, a brand new property is familiar ground, not a first attempt.

03

Falling supply

With national deliveries projected to fall from 1,205 facilities in 2023 to 455 by 2029, new competitors in this market are less and less likely.

04

Proven tech-stack

Fast online rentals, seamless self service move-ins, convenient smart entry and access. Systems handle everything that doesn’t require empathy. Humans handle everything that does.

05

Aligned capital

The Sponsors are co-investing 10% of its own equity alongside you, signing on the debt, and is paid a promote only after your preferred return is paid and original capital is returned.

Years one and two run below debt service. That is the plan, and it is funded.

A building bought empty does not cover its interest in year one. The $850,000 raised at close covers the gap while the loan is interest-only for 36 months. By Year 3 the property covers its debt 1.65 times over; Year 4 is stabilized. Read the negative Year 1 as a funded lease-up, not a surprise.
